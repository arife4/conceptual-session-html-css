The cow is a common farm animal that lives with people in villages and towns. It has four legs, two ears, two horns, a big nose, and a long tail with hair at the end. Its body is covered with short hair, which can be white, black, brown, or a mix of colors. Cows are herbivorous animals. They eat green grass, dry straw, leaves, husks, and grains. They are very helpful to humans. A cow gives us fresh and nutritious milk every day, which we use to make butter, curd, cheese, and ghee. Furthermore, cow dung is widely used as a rich natural fertilizer for crops and as cooking fuel in rural areas. Because of its calm nature and many benefits, people love and care for the cow.
